What I do

I help turn partner-led chaos into an operation that can scale.

I work alongside owners and leadership teams to identify what is slowing the firm down, create the operating structure it needs, and help the team execute. That may mean clarifying accountability, building KPIs, improving leadership meetings, fixing intake and case workflows, developing managers, implementing core processes or leading a major operational initiative.

The goal is not to make your firm dependent on me. The goal is an operation that performs more consistently because the right systems, people and accountability are in place.
