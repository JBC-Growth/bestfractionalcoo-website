Common questions

Before you book

How many hours do I get?

Fractional COO engagements aren't structured as a block of consulting hours. We agree on the responsibilities, cadence, priorities and outcomes I'm accountable for. If you mainly need hourly task support, I'm probably not the right solution.

Why not just hire a full-time COO?

If your firm truly needs a full-time executive and is ready for that investment, you should. A fractional model makes sense when you need COO-level leadership but not 40 hours a week, or when you want to build the operating structure before making a permanent executive hire.

Can we start smaller?

Yes. Most firms start with the Operational Health Assessment. If ongoing executive guidance is the better fit, we can talk about an advisory arrangement. I'd rather reduce the scope than water down the work.

Can you guarantee results?

I'm very clear about the work, the operating measures and what I will own. I can't responsibly guarantee a specific financial result, because execution also depends on the firm, its people and the decisions leadership makes.

Do you work with firms outside a specific practice area?

Yes. The operational challenges of growth look similar across practice areas: intake, case workflow, management, reporting and client experience.

Do you provide legal, tax or accounting advice?

No. My work is operational leadership. Legal, tax, accounting and specialized technical work stay with your existing advisors unless separately agreed.
