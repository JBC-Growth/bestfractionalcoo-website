Law firm operations

Your law firm may have outgrown the way it operates.

As firms grow, the owner can no longer personally solve every people, process, intake, technology, financial and client-experience issue.

I help growing law firms build the operational structure and leadership accountability they need to scale. I have led operational work inside complex, multi-location firm environments, so I know how a firm actually runs between the consult and the closed file.

Sound familiar?

What I hear from managing partners

  • The managing partner is still the operational bottleneck.
  • Intake, case management and administrative teams operate in silos.
  • Managers have titles but inconsistent management systems.
  • KPIs exist but aren't consistently used to drive decisions.
  • SOPs exist but adoption is inconsistent.
  • Client communication varies by team or employee.
  • Technology is underused or creates duplicate work.
  • Growth has added headcount without enough structure.
  • Leadership spends too much time reacting and not enough time operating strategically.
